## ATLAS-IDENTITY-NARROW-ADDED-DEPS — Narrow the source-identity clean when a run adds dependencies [arch] — todo
- **outcome:** a gate whose manifest change adds dependencies cleans and holds exclusive only its own package, the packages whose snapshot entry changed, and their in-closure dependents; packages new to the closure are held shared and left unrecorded (ADR 0064's rule for unnamed closure packages), so peers sharing the rest of the closure keep their artifacts and their shared leases.
- **evidence (2026-10-01, basis `4cbd966b6`):** a ritk-codecs gate adding `coeus-core` and `eunomia` grew its closure from 6 to 35 packages; two rules each forced a whole-closure clean — `_path_only_dependency_diff` gives up when `clean_packages` changes, and `_path_repository_safe_to_narrow` refuses any `Cargo.toml`/`Cargo.lock` path. It held 35 scopes exclusive through `cargo clean -p` x35 (over 5 min) and its clippy command; a metis push and an athena push each failed after the 900 s lease wait behind it, and cleaned artifacts turned later readers into writers. Lease timeline and queue dump: judge report in the session that filed this item.
- **verified premises:** `cargo clean -p X` removes every metadata-hash variant of X (probe: both feature variants and fingerprints removed), so exclusive-for-clean stays; Cargo reads `[profile]`, `[patch]`, `[replace]` only from the workspace root manifest (Cargo reference, Profiles and Overriding Dependencies), so a parsed root-table comparison can replace the filename rule for member manifests.
- **acceptance:** a test replaying the 6 -> 35 package addition narrows to the added edge's dependents, not the closure; the recorded counterexamples (features-only change, root `[profile.dev]` edit) still clean the whole closure.
- **priority:** tightening
- **needs:** ADR 0064 revision (its lines 57 and 59 record the decision this overturns)
- **scope:** `scripts/atlas_build_identity.py`, `scripts/tests/test_atlas_build_identity.py`, `docs/adr/0064-shared-build-source-identity.md`
- **next:** draft the ADR 0064 revision with the narrowing rule and the root-table comparison, then implement against the replay test.
