## ATLAS-IDENTITY-LEASE-STARVATION - Gated pushes starve on the 900 s identity-lease wait [verification] - todo
- priority: verification
- basis: 1b92f219b
- outcome: a gated member push under fleet load waits at most one peer gate step for a source-identity lease. It is never refused for a lease another push holds through a whole gate.
- evidence (2026-09-30): eight serial pushes were refused after the 900 s wait (helios, ritk, hephaestus, kwavers, metis, CFDrs, and #408 four times), and on 2026-10-01 the hephaestus and both ritk pushes again. In the hephaestus refusal, a ritk gate held `hephaestus-rocm` at ritk's revision.
- measured (2026-10-02, 12 gates in flight): up to eight identity runs waited at once with one Cargo running. Each took its leases one at a time in name order and kept the early ones while it waited on a later one: a leto run held `mnemosyne-heap` and `mnemosyne-arena` shared for 13 minutes, and the mnemosyne gate that needed them exclusive waited 757 s of the 900 s bound. One 12-package metis step holds exclusive for about 12 minutes. Delivered by the claim protocol (ADR 0064): a run takes all its leases together and waits holding none.
- cross-member thrash (refuted for records, confirmed for cleaning): records exist only for a run's gated packages, so members never share one. 17 of 210 gated-package leases were last held exclusive by a member that does not gate the package (`cargo clean -p` of a Git package, which is by name), so cleans do cross members. Cargo 1.97.0 names a path package's artifacts without the checkout's path, so artifacts cannot be keyed apart without a per-source target; same-repository gates serialize in the hook instead.
- acceptance (corrected): (1) a test with two runs on one closure records the second run's wait as bounded by the first run's hold of that step; (2) the pre-push hook refuses a second concurrent push of the same ref. A push of another ref of the repository is not made to wait: the identity claims already exclude conflicting build work, and serializing whole gates moves the starvation to a new lock (a 12-package metis gate runs about 36 minutes; ADR 0064); (3) every claim records its wait and hold per lease. Wall time before and after ATLAS-GATE-STEP-BATCH at isolated cores is not obtainable on a fleet host with 12 gates in flight, so the 36-to-3 reduction is counted from the hook's identity-run calls instead.
