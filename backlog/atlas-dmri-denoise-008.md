## ATLAS-DMRI-DENOISE-008 — MP-PCA denoising and Gibbs unringing [minor] — in-progress
- outcome: `ritk-filter` gains Marchenko-Pastur PCA denoising and subvoxel Gibbs ringing removal — the `dwidenoise`/`mrdegibbs` roles, the first two stages of every dMRI pipeline. Neither exists today: current denoisers (bilateral, patch, median, rank, anisotropic-diffusion) are not the MP-PCA estimator, and `noise_estimation.rs` is MAD over Gaussian noise, not the Rician/noncentral-chi model magnitude DWI follows.
- delivered: MP-PCA as `ritk_filter::mppca::MpPcaDenoiser` in [ritk#623](https://github.com/ryancinsight/ritk/pull/623) — Veraart 2016 MP threshold with the Cordero-Grande 2019 refinement as default, sigma and rank maps, verified on a synthesized rank-3 field with known sigma. Gibbs unringing as `ritk_filter::gibbs_unringing::GibbsUnringer` in [ritk#625](https://github.com/ryancinsight/ritk/pull/625) — Kellner 2016 subvoxel shifts on apollo-fft, ringing reduced 19x on a truncated step edge. next: move both single-threaded sweeps onto the stack's parallel provider, and compare against MRtrix `dwidenoise`/`mrdegibbs` output on real DWI.
- related: Rician bias correction splits out as its own item if MP-PCA outgrows its acceptance criteria.
